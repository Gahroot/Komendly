# Layout starters

Pick the style that fits the business and the request; vary it. Copy to ./index.html, then adapt SIZE marks, palette, copy, images and SCENES timing.

- illustrated (../starter.html): friendly 4-scene promo with kit illustrations (hook, offer, deal, end card); any business with no footage. 9:16, 1:1, 16:9. Images: none (kit illustrations).
- bold-type.html: kinetic giant-type statement ad, outline/scrolling words, big stat; brands with attitude, launches, slogans. 9:16, 1:1, 16:9. Images: none.
- product-showcase.html: one product card on a clean studio backdrop with callouts and price badge; e-commerce, physical products. 9:16, 1:1, 16:9. Images: 1-2 product photos (falls back to illustrations).
- offer-price.html: loud sale ad: headline, counting % off with burst, countdown, old vs new price, CTA; discounts, promos, limited-time deals. 9:16, 1:1, 16:9. Images: optional product image.
- before-after.html: before/after reveal with a sliding divider, then side by side; cleaning, repairs, beauty, renovation, fitness. 9:16, 1:1, 16:9. Images: a before and an after photo (falls back to illustrations).
- testimonial.html: customer quote with stars and name; social proof for services, clinics, restaurants. 9:16 only. Images: optional customer/avatar photo.
- listicle.html: numbered tips or reasons, one per scene; educational content, "3 reasons to...". 9:16 only. Images: optional, one per item.
- caption-ugc.html: phone-native look with bold word-by-word captions over footage or a photo; UGC-style social ads, works with uploaded footage. 9:16 only. Images: 1+ lifestyle photos.
- editorial.html: elegant serif magazine layout; premium, hospitality, fashion, real estate. 9:16, 1:1. Images: 1-3 high-quality photos.
- app-feature.html: browser window with a screenshot, a cursor that glides and taps, zoom-in on the part that matters, 2 feature beats, CTA with URL; apps, SaaS, websites, launch videos. 16:9 (set SIZE to 1920x1080) and 9:16. Images: 1 screenshot (./brand/photos or their uploads; falls back to kit/illustrations/laptop-dashboard.svg).
- local-service.html: trust-led local business ad (service, area, rating, call/book CTA); plumbers, dentists, salons, trades. 9:16. Images: optional work/team photo.
