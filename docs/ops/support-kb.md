## Downloading your ads (`download`)

Open your pack from your Komendly dashboard and use the Download button on each ad. 10 free ads from your website, no card needed (20 if a friend's referral link sent you); some ads in a pack stay locked until you unlock it. If a download fails for an ad that is unlocked, reply with the email you signed up with and we will fix it.

## Locked ads (`locked`)

The rest of the pack unlocks with any plan or a one-time $9 "Unlock this pack". After that, every ad in that pack is downloadable.

## Charges and billing (`billing`)

Ozzy's AI Marketing Team (Growth Autopilot): $297/month founding price, locked for as long as you stay subscribed (regular price $497/month). Do-it-yourself: Ozzy Ad Department is $197/month. Ad spend is paid by you directly to Meta, never through Komendly, and never goes over the monthly limit you set. Your receipts are emailed by Stripe and listed under Billing in your account.

## Refunds (`refund`)

There is a 30-day refund guarantee on your first payment: you can use "Refund my first payment" under Billing, or reply and say why it did not work for you. We can't promise a number of leads or sales: that depends on your offer, your area and your market.

## Connecting Meta (`meta_setup`)

For the done-for-you plan we set up your Meta connection with you: you give partner access to your Page and ad account from Meta Business Settings, and we never see your password. If you are stuck on a step, reply with a screenshot of where you are and we will walk you through it. Ad spend always stays in your own Meta account.

## Cancelling (`cancel`)

You can cancel any time under Billing in your account; the plan then stays active until the end of the period you paid for. Cancelling does not delete your ads.

## The free pack (`free_pack`)

10 free ads from your website, no card needed (20 if a friend's referral link sent you). Paste your website link on the Komendly home page and the ads are made from your own site.

## Results and leads (`results`)

We can't promise a number of leads or sales: that depends on your offer, your area and your market. What we do guarantee is the work: fresh ads made from your website, and for the done-for-you plan, ads launched and watched for you.
