# Komendly for Claude

Komendly makes ready-to-run Facebook and Instagram ads for a local business from its website. Give Claude your site's address: Komendly reads your brand, offer and audience and makes an Ad Pack of static ads (image, headline, ad copy and call to action) sized for Meta feeds and stories. The ads appear in an interactive gallery right in the chat, with Download, Publish to Meta and Upgrade buttons. Publishing happens on komendly.com, where you connect your Meta ad account and approve the budget; nothing is spent from the chat.

## What's inside

- **Connector** (`.mcp.json`): Komendly's hosted MCP server, `https://www.komendly.com/api/mcp` (OAuth sign-in; new accounts are created during sign-in).
- **Skill** `komendly-meta-ads`: the steps Claude follows to make, review and publish Meta ads for a local business.

## Pricing

New accounts get one free Ad Pack with 3 ads (no card). More ads need a paid plan; Ozzy Ad Department is $97 for the first month, then $197/month. See https://www.komendly.com/pricing.
