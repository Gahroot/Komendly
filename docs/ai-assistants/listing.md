**Description (≤2000, Claude; reused in plugin.json for ChatGPT):**
Komendly turns a business website into ready-to-run ads. Paste your site's address and Komendly reads your brand, offer and audience, then makes an Ad Pack of static ads (image, headline and ad copy) sized for Facebook, Instagram and other social feeds and stories. Ozzy, Komendly's video maker, also makes short promo, launch and explainer videos from a prompt and edits them on request ("make it 9:16", "add captions").

For small business owners, marketers and founders who need ad creatives without a designer. Connect, sign in or create a free Komendly account, then ask in chat. New accounts get 10 free ads from their website (no card) and one free Ozzy video; more uses credits on a paid plan, and Komendly links to pricing when needed.

In chat, Komendly makes creatives and copy; it never publishes ads or spends ad budget from the chat. Websites must be public. Ads take a few minutes; videos a few more.

**Use cases:** make social ads from a website · make a promo/launch video · write Meta ad copy · get a hand-off kit for an ads tool.
**Needs:** a free Komendly account (created during sign-in). **Reads and writes:** both (creates Ad Packs and videos on the user's account; never deletes).
