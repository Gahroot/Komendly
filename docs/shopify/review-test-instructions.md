# Testing instructions for Shopify App Review (paste into App submission > Testing instructions)

No login or external account is needed: installing the app creates the Komendly account automatically.

1. Install the app on your test store and accept the `read_products` permission. The app opens inside the Shopify admin.
2. Home shows "Free ads from your store". Click **Make free ads from my store**, or click **Make ads** on any product card (the store needs at least one active product published to the Online Store).
3. A status card appears ("Ozzy is making your ads"). Generation takes about 3-8 minutes; the card updates on its own.
4. When ready, click **Open my ads**: a new tab opens the finished ad pack in Komendly (signed in automatically). Download any ad.
5. Billing: in **Plans (billed by Shopify)** click **Choose** on Starter. Approve the (test) charge on Shopify's screen. You return to the app; the plan shows as current with 60 credits. Choose the other plan to test switching; the first subscription is replaced.
6. Uninstall from Settings > Apps and sales channels. Access tokens are deleted immediately.

Notes: the free pack is one per business (anti-abuse). A development store's charges are always test charges.
Contact during review: the emergency developer contact on the Partner account.
