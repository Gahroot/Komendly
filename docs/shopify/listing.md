# App Store listing (paste into Partner Dashboard > Apps > Komendly > Distribution > App listing)

Rules this copy follows: no prices, reviews or Shopify trademarks outside the Pricing section; factual claims only.

**App name:** Komendly: Ad Creatives  (must match `name` in shopify.app.toml)

**App introduction (≤100 chars):**
Turn your store and products into ready-to-post ad creatives in minutes. First pack free.

**App details (≤500 chars):**
Komendly reads your storefront and products and makes a pack of on-brand ad creatives for Facebook, Instagram and TikTok: static ads in feed and story sizes plus an animated ad, with headlines and captions written for your offer. Pick your whole store or a single product, press one button, and download the finished ads. No design skills, no ad account access, nothing added to your theme.

**Feature list (each ≤80 chars):**
1. Ad packs made from your own store and product pages
2. Feed (4:5, 1:1) and story (9:16) sizes, plus an animated ad
3. Headlines and captions written for your products and audience
4. Pick one product or your whole store, one click to start
5. Read-only access to products; nothing changes on your storefront

**Pricing (Pricing details section):**
- Free: first ad pack free (one per business).
- Starter: $49 / 30 days, 60 credits a month for ad packs and Studio videos.
- Ozzy Ad Department: $197 / 30 days, 20x Starter usage, weekly fresh ads.
Billed by Shopify. Change or cancel any time inside the app.
